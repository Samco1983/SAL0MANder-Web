# SAL0MANder Epic Puzzle Soundtrack — Creative Brief v2

## Direction
Original game music with:
- cinematic JRPG adventure feeling
- gothic arcade-survival momentum
- puzzle-game clarity and loopability
- strong memorable motifs
- dramatic progression without copying any existing franchise melody, arrangement, or recording

Reference qualities only: adventurous orchestral fantasy, dark castle energy, escalating survival-game pulse, heroic victory payoff.

## Musical identity
**SAL0MANder motif:** a short rising 4-note idea that can reappear in menu, puzzle, boss/challenge, and victory tracks in different forms.

Suggested base motif scale degrees: **1 – 2 – ♭3 – 5**, then resolve upward to **6 or 1** depending on mood.

Use different keys/modes so it feels like one game world, not one repeated song.

---

## Track 1 — SAL0MANder Overture
**Use:** title screen / main menu / brand intro  
**Mood:** mysterious → adventurous → heroic  
**Tempo:** 92 BPM  
**Meter:** 6/8  
**Mode:** D Dorian moving into D minor / brief major lift  
**Length:** 75–100 sec seamless loop

**Instrumentation**
- piano opening
- low strings
- French-horn style brass
- taiko-like cinematic drums
- harp/plucked sparkle
- restrained choir pad, no lyrics

**Structure**
1. 0:00–0:16 — lone piano states the SAL0MANder motif
2. 0:16–0:36 — strings answer it
3. 0:36–0:58 — drums + brass turn it heroic
4. 0:58–1:16 — full statement, then reduce back into loop point

**Rule:** memorable but not bombastic enough to tire users on the menu.

---

## Track 2 — Puzzle Hunters
**Use:** normal jigsaw / Learning Puzzle gameplay  
**Mood:** clever, adventurous, forward-moving  
**Tempo:** 118 BPM  
**Meter:** 4/4  
**Mode:** E Dorian  
**Length:** 90–120 sec loop

**Instrumentation**
- pizzicato strings
- arpeggiated synth/pluck
- hand percussion
- light kick
- bass ostinato
- occasional brass swell

**Gameplay feel**
The piece should make dragging puzzle pieces feel like exploring a dungeon room rather than doing homework.

**Dynamic rule:** keep 1–3 kHz uncluttered so correct-answer / snap / reveal SFX remain readable.

---

## Track 3 — Midnight Puzzle Castle
**Use:** harder levels / challenge mode / dark image packs  
**Mood:** gothic, urgent, addictive  
**Tempo:** 142 BPM  
**Mode:** A harmonic minor  
**Length:** 90 sec loop

**Instrumentation**
- pipe-organ-inspired synth
- rapid string ostinato
- driving bass
- toms
- bell accents
- distorted-but-clean low synth pulse

**Structure**
- 8-bar ominous intro
- 16-bar driving A section
- 16-bar higher-register B section
- 8-bar tension bridge
- return to A with denser percussion

This is the closest lane to arcade survival energy: constant forward pressure, but not sonic chaos.

---

## Track 4 — Pieces Under Pressure
**Use:** timer mode / near-completion / optional high-energy mode  
**Mood:** urgent without panic  
**Tempo:** 156 BPM  
**Mode:** C minor  
**Length:** 60–75 sec loop

**Core idea**
A repeating 8th-note ostinato gradually gains layers while the harmony changes underneath it.

**Instrumentation**
- low strings
- pulse synth
- orchestral percussion
- short brass stabs
- clock/tick texture used very sparingly

**Do not:** add an actual countdown sound unless gameplay owns the timing cue.

---

## Track 5 — The Picture Awakens
**Use:** final reveal / puzzle completion  
**Mood:** triumph + wonder  
**Tempo:** 108 BPM  
**Length:** 18–30 sec non-looping sting

**Instrumentation**
- full strings
- brass
- cymbal swell
- glockenspiel/bell sparkle
- choir pad without words

**Shape**
Start with the SAL0MANder motif in minor, then resolve it in a brighter harmony for the final image reveal.

---

## Track 6 — Victory of the Little Salamander
**Use:** major milestone / first completion / campaign reward  
**Mood:** full RPG victory theme  
**Tempo:** 132 BPM  
**Length:** 25–40 sec

**Hook**
A bold transformation of the 4-note motif into a major-key fanfare.

**Instrumentation**
- brass lead
- snare
- strings
- timpani
- sparkle layer

Short enough that students want to hear it again.

---

## Track 7 — Boss Puzzle: The Last Four Pieces
**Use:** optional final-stage/challenge mode  
**Mood:** dramatic, slightly ridiculous, fun  
**Tempo:** 168 BPM  
**Mode:** D minor with chromatic passing harmony  
**Length:** 90 sec loop

**Instrumentation**
- aggressive strings
- rock drums
- electric bass
- orchestral hits
- organ
- synth arpeggio

The joke is that a four-piece puzzle is being treated like the final boss—but the music should still sound genuinely good.

---

## Adaptive music idea
Later, gameplay can use stems:
- **Base:** rhythm + harmony
- **Discovery:** plucks / bells
- **Pressure:** percussion + ostinato
- **Final 25%:** brass/string lift
- **Victory:** completion sting

This allows intensity to rise as the puzzle gets closer to completion without restarting the song.

---

## Audio production requirements
- 44.1 or 48 kHz WAV master
- game-ready OGG derivative where needed
- integrated loudness target roughly consistent across the pack
- no clipping
- clean loop points
- no copyrighted samples without documented license
- no imitation of recognizable melodies
- music must duck slightly under important gameplay feedback
- mute state always respected
- browser playback begins only after a valid user gesture where required

## Acceptance
A track is worth shipping if:
- it sounds like a real game soundtrack, not generic study background
- the first 10 seconds establish character
- the loop can run three times without becoming irritating
- puzzle SFX remain clear over it
- it is fully original and provenance is recorded
