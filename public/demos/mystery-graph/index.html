How to play: graph, fly and face the boss

On the coordinate grid

Tap or drag to a grid intersection and release to check it. Or use the on-screen x − / x + / y − / y + buttons, then Lock point.

With a keyboard, use the arrow keys to move and Enter to lock. Undo (or Backspace) removes the last point. A wrong point leaves your picture intact.

Every square is one unit. Some shapes close automatically after their last point. Start a fresh line when prompted.

Save your creation

After completing the graph, break the purple glass to restore your creation's true colors. Fly in all directions with WASD / arrow keys or drag. Regular shots fire automatically. Hold the mouse button, Z or the charge button, then release to blast. Space or Pause pauses the fight.

Easy starts with four shields and 12 seconds of larger, stronger rapid shots. Hard awards 2×; Very Hard adds denser attacks and awards 3×. Easy has two emergency bombs; other difficulties have one. Press X or Bomb to clear incoming fire. Use Missions → Music to choose separate plotting and battle tracks.

Major shield breaks freeze the arena and overlay a coordinate grid. Enter the numbered weak-point coordinates to launch a targeted strike, or skip back to flying. There is no countdown. Each broken glass section drops a power-up. Move to catch a cyan gem for larger, stronger rapid shots or a green gem for a shield. Break the whole shell to save the creation.

Back closes the game. Your graph and earned progress stay in this browser; an unfinished battle restarts when you return.

Play upright or sideways. 10 levels: whole numbers in Quadrant I; integers in Quadrants I–II, I–IV and all four quadrants.
