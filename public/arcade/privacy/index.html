Privacy and safety

The SAL0MANder games are free and made for kids. We built them so there is nothing about your child to collect, sell or leak.

For kids

  • Nobody asks your name. You race as a cartoon face you pick or build.
  • There is no chat and no messages. Nobody can talk to you in the games.
  • Your best runs and your face are saved only on the device you play on.
  • Never type your name, address, school or anything personal anywhere. The games never need it.

For parents

  • No accounts, no names, no email. Children never sign up and never give personal information.
  • No photos, no camera, no microphone. Online, players appear only as cartoon faces built from parts, sent between devices as a single number. We never collect, upload, scan or measure a child's face, photo or voice.
  • No chat. Online races use a 4-letter room code. Other players see only a cartoon face, a seat number and the race.
  • Nothing stored on our side. The race server passes game moves along and keeps nothing. A room disappears when the last player leaves.
  • Saved on your device only: best runs, the chosen face, sound and control settings, and a halfway checkpoint. You can delete them any time with the button below, or in each game.
  • No ads, no ad tracking, no selling of data.
  • We follow the federal children's privacy law (COPPA) and apply the strictest state rules (including face and biometric data laws) to every player.

Free-time play with Coach Sam

Coach Sam (Samuel Saldivar) tutors math through SAL0MANder Math. His students may race him in the games outside tutoring hours only with a parent's written OK. The same rules apply: no chat, no names, no photos, nothing stored.

Delete saved game data on this device

This removes best runs, faces, checkpoints and settings for every SAL0MANder game in this browser.

Questions

Parents can ask anything about privacy, or ask us to confirm we hold nothing about their child, through salomandermath.com. We answer within 30 days.
